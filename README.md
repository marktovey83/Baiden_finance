# Baiden Finance

A simple fortnightly money planner. Open `index.html` in any browser. There's nothing to install and no login.

1. **Pay**: pick the pay date and enter pay before tax and tax paid to get take-home pay.
2. **Split**: take-home split 60% expenses, 20% spending, 20% savings, with account name, BSB and account number for each.
3. **Bills**: add bills as a direct debit or with a due date, set how often, and mark them paid. Recurring bills roll on to their next date.
4. **Savings**: deposits, withdrawals, add this fortnight's 20% in one click, and a savings goal.
5. **Debts**: credit cards, loans, Afterpay and so on, with amount owing, repayments, progress and bills still to pay before next pay day.

Data is saved in your browser (localStorage) on this device only.
