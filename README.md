# Baiden Finance

A simple fortnightly money planner. Open `index.html` in any browser. There's nothing to install and no login.

1. **Pay**: pick the pay date and enter pay before tax and tax paid to get take-home pay. Press *Save this pay* to add it to your pay history.
2. **Split**: take-home split 60% expenses, 20% spending, 20% savings, with account name, BSB and account number for each.
3. **Bills / Direct Debits**: add bills as a direct debit or with a due date, set how often, and mark them paid. Recurring bills roll on to their next date.
4. **Savings**: deposits, withdrawals, add this fortnight's 20% in one click, and a savings goal.
5. **Debts**: credit cards, loans, Afterpay and so on, with amount owing and repayments (kept separate from the 60% expenses), progress and bills still to pay before next pay day.
6. **Totals**: a running total of every saved pay (plus this financial year), where you stand overall (savings minus debts and bills due), a fortnightly breakdown, fortnight/month/year totals, and savings and debt progress so far.

Data is saved in your browser (localStorage) on this device only.
