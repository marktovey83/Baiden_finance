# Baiden Finance

A simple fortnightly money planner. Open `index.html` in any browser. There's nothing to install and no login.

- **Pay**: enter your fortnightly pay before tax and the tax paid to see your take-home pay.
- **Split**: take-home is split 60% expenses, 20% spending, 20% savings.
- **Bills & Direct Debits**: add bills with an amount, frequency and due date. Mark them paid, and recurring bills roll on to their next due date.
- **Savings**: track deposits and withdrawals, add this fortnight's 20% in one click, and set a goal.

Data is saved in your browser (localStorage) on this device only.
